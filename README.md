Adapts code seen in (https://glitch.com/edit/#!/bu-hands3?path=style.css%3A22%3A0) for BU AI Art Mini-Hack 2025
Instruction:
Download everything
Open index.html in your browser and it will run a webcam
Click Allow the webcam use
Make sure both of your hands are in the frame, only one person can draw at a time
Pinch your left hand with different fingers combo to change color (you can try on your own)
Pinch your right hand as a pen and move freely to draw on the canvas
When you finish, you can raise both of your hands to save the png of what you draw
